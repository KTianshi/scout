Scout_□✕

A helper for your computer that watches out for scams.

Scout sits next to your mouse. Ask it how to do something and it points to the right button, one step at a time. If a page or message looks like a scam, it tells you before you click.

Download for Mac
Free · macOS 26 · Apple silicon
What Scout does
  • Shows you where to clickHold ⌥ Space or say “Hey Scout,” then ask. “How do I attach a photo?”
  • Warns you about scamsFake virus alerts, gift-card demands, “your grandson is in jail” messages. Scout points at the problem and explains it.
  • Slows down risky paymentsBefore money or a password goes to a page that looks wrong, Scout asks you to pause.
How to install
  1. Unzip it and drag Scout into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click Scout and choose Open.
  3. Allow the screen and microphone when it asks.
  4. Add your Claude key to ~/.config/scout/secrets.env as ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=…
