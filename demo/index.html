Lookout demo scenarios

Six scams that target older adults, and four normal pages that look a little scary but are fine. Press a number key to open one.

Scams should warn

1Fake virus alert
"Windows Defender" pop-up says the computer is infected and to call support now.
threat: phone · micros0ft-security-alert.com
2IRS gift-card demand
Email threatens arrest unless $1,500 is paid in gift cards, read over the phone.
threat: gift-card · irs-gov-payments.co
3"Grandson in jail"
Text from "Tyler" asking for $3,000 bail by wire or Zelle. "Don't tell Mom and Dad."
threat: wire · unknown number
4Fake bank sign-in
"Account locked" page on a lookalike domain asks for password, SSN and card.
threat: login · …verify-account.net
5Refund + remote access
"$399.99 renewal refund" page says to download AnyDesk and read the code to a technician.
threat: remote-app · support-desk.live
6Romance crypto investment
Online "friend" Linda pushes guaranteed 30% monthly returns and a Bitcoin ATM deposit.
threat: crypto · bitgrowth-pro.io
